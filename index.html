Update available
Your Blockhaven app is outdated.
Or update by hand:
  1. Open Blockhaven from your apps or home screen.
  2. On the home screen, tap the version number (bottom left).
  3. Wait for it to say Updating, then the game reloads.
Still old? Close the app fully and open it again.
Install the game to play it. It works offline once installed.
On iPhone / iPad:
  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Choose "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Open Blockhaven from your home screen.
No install button?
  1. Open your browser menu.
  2. Choose "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Open Blockhaven from your apps.
