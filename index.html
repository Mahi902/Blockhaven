About the game

A free-form apartment sandbox. No quests, no rules. Just a blank space and everything you need to make it yours.

Build

Drag out rooms, raise walls, add doors and windows. Floors, walls and ceilings are yours to color.

Decorate

Beds, sofas, TVs, arcades and more. Every piece comes in a pile of colors.

Live

Walk around, sit, sleep, use things, and talk. Save as many worlds as you like.

Together

Hang out with friends and trade emotes: hugs, handshakes, high fives.
